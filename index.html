    Create a market

    Anyone can open a market for a Pyth price feed and earns 0.1% of its settled volume. New markets start unverified.

    How it works

    1. 1

      Pick a side

      Every round is one minute. Stake on Rise if you think the price ends above the strike, or Fall if below.

    2. 2

      The strike locks

      When betting closes, the price is locked as the strike: the bright line across the chart. From here, watch it move.

    3. 3

      Winners split the pool

      Everyone on the winning side shares the whole pool, less a 1.5% fee, in proportion to their stake. The multiplier on each side is your payout per 1 MON right now, and it moves as others bet.

    4. 4

      No contest, no loss

      If nobody took the other side, or the price closes exactly at the strike, every stake is refunded in full.

    Prices come from Pyth, the same oracle the contract settles on. Every bet and payout is public in the Dashboard. Demo uses practice MON and simulated players; Live uses your wallet on Monad.
